Travel & visa

From the airport

Hong Kong International Airport is on Lantau Island, west of the city. Three straightforward options connect the airport to Wan Chai.

Airport Express

The Airport Express runs to Hong Kong Station in about 24 minutes. From there, take a taxi or the MTR to Wan Chai. Through-check-in is available at some stations. See the Airport Express information.

Taxi

A direct taxi from the airport to Wan Chai takes about 40–60 minutes, depending on traffic. Use a red urban taxi from the official taxi rank; red urban taxis serve the airport and all of Hong Kong Island. Keep the receipt and pay the metered fare plus tunnel and luggage charges.

Airport bus

Take Citybus A11 from the airport bus terminus towards North Point Ferry Pier. It runs through Central, Admiralty and Wan Chai. Alight at Fleming Road or Wan Chai Fire Station on Hennessy Road, then walk about 8–12 minutes to {{venue.name}}. The journey takes about 70 minutes; pay by Octopus or contactless card. Check the Citybus A11 route and stop before boarding.

From the mainland and Macau

If you are travelling from the mainland or Macau, high-speed rail and ferry routes both connect to Wan Chai by MTR or taxi.

High-speed rail

High-speed rail services arrive at West Kowloon Station. Clear immigration and customs at the station, then continue to Wan Chai by MTR or taxi. Follow the station signs to the connected MTR network, or use the official taxi rank. Allow extra time for border clearance at peak periods.

Ferries

Ferries from Macau and the mainland arrive at the China Ferry Terminal in Tsim Sha Tsui or the Hong Kong–Macau Ferry Terminal in Sheung Wan. From Sheung Wan, the Island Line runs directly to Wan Chai. From Tsim Sha Tsui, continue by taxi or MTR.

  • Keep your passport, entry permit or other travel documents to hand for immigration.
  • Allow extra time at West Kowloon Station and the ferry terminals during peak periods.
  • An Octopus card works on the MTR, buses and ferries; contactless bank cards are accepted on the MTR.

To the venue by MTR

Wan Chai Station (Island Line, Exit D) is about a 3–5 minute walk via Lee Tung Street and Hopewell Mall. From Exit D, head towards Queen's Road East, enter Hopewell Mall and take the lift to the hotel lobby on 19/F. Exhibition Centre Station (East Rail Line) is about a 10-minute walk. Use the lift exits where available and follow the hotel signage.

An Octopus card works on the MTR, buses, ferries and many shops. Single-journey tickets and contactless bank cards are also accepted on the MTR.

By bus or minibus

From Central or Admiralty, Citybus routes 6, 6X, 10, 15 and 66 run east along Queen's Road East. From Causeway Bay, green minibus 28 or 28S runs via Kennedy Road.

Taxi and parking

Ask the driver for {{venue.name}}, {{venue.street}}. Red urban taxis serve all of Hong Kong Island. Vehicles use the Kennedy Road entrance and arrive at Level 18; take the lift or escalator to the hotel lobby on 19/F. The public car park is on 14/F–16/F of the Hopewell Mall in the same building; parking fees apply. See the Hopewell Mall parking details.

Accessibility

The venue is wheelchair accessible and lifts serve the meeting floors. Crystal Court is on 19/F; the Salons are on 20/F and are reached by Lift L1-8 from 19/F. If you arrive by taxi or car, use the Level 18 arrival hall and take the lift to 19/F. If you need assistance with transport, access or seating, tell the registration team before the meeting. See Accessibility for more.

Visa requirements

Visa requirements depend on your nationality and the length of your stay. Check the Hong Kong Immigration Department for the official list. {{event.shortName}} cannot advise on individual visa eligibility.

If you need an invitation letter, send your details to the Meeting Secretariat. Letters are issued by the meeting secretariat and usually take up to 10 working days. An invitation letter does not guarantee a visa.

Request an invitation letter

Send your full name as in your passport, nationality, role, institution, and arrival and departure dates to the Meeting Secretariat. Allow up to 10 working days.
