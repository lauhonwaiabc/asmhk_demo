Formal Project Prize

Internal College process

From Formal Project to Prize Session

Invited presenters present in the Formal Project Prize Session. The confirmed date, time and room are listed in Key dates below; the session page lists the session details and submitted abstracts.

View the session and submitted abstracts Enquiries

Who is considered

  • HKCA trainees who have submitted a Formal Project through the College process.
  • The project must have completed the internal audit and review steps required by the College.
  • Presenters are invited after the Formal Project Officer has considered the recommendations.
  • Presenters must be registered for {{event.shortName}}.

Internal audit and recommendation

  1. The submitted Formal Project is received and checked by the College.
  2. The project is audited internally for completeness, methodology and documentation.
  3. The audit outcome and recommendation are forwarded to the Formal Project Officer.
  4. The Formal Project Officer confirms the shortlist and invitations are issued.
  5. Invited presenters are scheduled into the Formal Project Prize Session.

Notes

  • Submitting to the Free Paper Presentation does not affect Formal Project evaluation.
  • The College's decision on the shortlist and prizes is final.
  • Queries about the internal process should go to the secretariat or the Formal Project Officer.
