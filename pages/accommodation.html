Accommodation

{{event.shortName}} delegates book directly with the hotel using the {{event.shortName}} booking code. This keeps the booking relationship between you and the hotel and lets you manage changes, cancellations and receipts directly.

How booking works

  1. Choose a hotel from the list when partners are published.
  2. Use the {{event.shortName}} booking code on the hotel's own booking page or by phone.
  3. Book before the cut-off date to receive the negotiated rate.
  4. Check the hotel's cancellation terms before you confirm.

Rates and availability

Negotiated nightly rates, room types and cut-off dates will be listed with each hotel. Rates are in Hong Kong dollars and exclude incidental charges unless stated.

Booking terms

Each hotel sets its own deposit, cancellation and no-show terms. The {{event.shortName}} code does not override the hotel's terms.
