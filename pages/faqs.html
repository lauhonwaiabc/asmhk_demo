General

What is {{event.shortName}}?

{{event.shortName}} is the Annual Scientific Meeting in Anaesthesiology, an international congress held on {{event.dateLabel}} at the {{venue.short}}. The theme is {{event.theme}}.

Who organises the meeting?

The meeting is organised by The Hong Kong College of Anaesthesiologists and The Society of Anaesthetists of Hong Kong.

What is the official language?

The official language of the meeting is English.

Is there a hashtag?

Yes. Use {{event.hashtag}} when posting about the meeting.

How do I know an email or website is official?

The official site is asmhk.org and the official enquiry route is the Contact page. We do not use third-party room-booking agents or ask you to pay by bank transfer to an individual. If you are unsure, check with us through the Contact page before you act.

Is the meeting in person or online?

ASM 2027 is planned as an in-person meeting in Hong Kong. Selected content is planned to be available after the meeting through OnDemand; there is no live stream during the meeting.

Is there an exhibition?

Yes. A healthcare industry exhibition runs alongside the scientific sessions, with twenty booths planned.

Venue and travel

Where is the venue?

{{venue.name}}, {{venue.street}}. The meeting uses Crystal Court on 19/F and the Salons on 20/F.

How do I get to the venue?

The hotel is a short walk from Wan Chai Station (Island Line) and Exhibition Centre Station (East Rail Line). Taxis and airport buses serve the hotel; see the Travel & visa page for details.

Is parking available?

Yes. The Hopewell Mall public car park is on 14/F–16/F of the same building. Parking fees apply.

Is there luggage storage for delegates?

Yes. Temporary luggage storage is available for delegates at the registration desk, 19/F, during meeting hours. Leave your luggage before the programme and collect it before you leave the venue. See Venue for delegate services.

Can I get a visa invitation letter?

Yes. See Travel & visa for invitation-letter details. The secretariat issues letters and aims to respond within 10 working days. A letter does not guarantee a visa.

Which floors does the meeting use?

The meeting uses Crystal Court on 19/F and the Salons on 20/F. Floor plans are on the Meeting Information page.

How do I get to the venue from the airport?

See Travel & visa for airport, MTR, taxi and parking options. The venue is at {{venue.street}}.

Registration

When does registration open?

Online registration opens on {{registration.opens}}. Early-bird registration closes on {{registration.earlyBirdDeadline}}.

What is included in registration?

Access to the scientific sessions on the days registered, the healthcare industry exhibition, tea breaks and lunches, the e-Programme and abstract book, and a certificate of attendance subject to the attendance requirement.

Can I register a group?

Group registration is available for hospital departments and institutions. Use the Contact page with the number of delegates and categories.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations must be made in writing. A 50% refund applies for cancellations on or before {{ePosterFreePaper.presentingDeadline}}. No refund is available after that date. Refunds are processed after the meeting.

Can I transfer my registration?

Substitutions are considered on written request. Use the Contact page before the meeting.

Are day registrations available?

Day rates are available for HKCA Fellows and non-HKCA Fellows. Trainee, student and intern categories register for the full meeting.

Are pre-conference workshops included in registration?

No. Optional workshops are booked separately and allocated on a first-come-first-served basis. Fees and quotas are listed on the Registration page.

Is there a nursing registration rate?

Yes. The Nursing Symposium has separate one-day and two-day rates; see the Registration page.

Abstracts

How do I submit an abstract?

Submit through the online abstract portal when it opens on {{registration.opens}}. The deadline is {{ePosterFreePaper.abstractDeadline}}. The abstract limit is 300 words with up to one table or figure.

Is every abstract accepted?

Every abstract that meets the guidelines is accepted and displayed as an e-Poster. At submission you choose e-Poster only, or e-Poster plus consideration for the Free Paper Presentation.

How does the Free Paper Presentation work?

Judges review all accepted e-Posters during the Day 1 e-Poster Evaluation Session. Only the best e-Posters whose authors chose Free Paper consideration at submission proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation. High-quality e-Posters that did not opt for Free Paper may still be invited to present in the Day 1 evaluation session, but they do not proceed to the Free Paper Presentation.

When will I hear about my abstract?

Notifications are sent to presenting authors from {{ePosterFreePaper.notification}}. Final format and presentation instructions are confirmed by email.

What do I upload for the e-Poster?

A single-page PDF in the supplied template, at 150 dpi or higher and no more than 20 MB. The file deadline is {{ePosterFreePaper.fileDeadline}}.

Can I submit more than one abstract?

Each presenting author may submit up to two abstracts. The presenting author must register as a paid delegate by the stated deadline.

Programme and app

When is the programme available?

The programme is published on this site. The e-Programme is published on {{eProgramme.date}}.

Do I need the event app?

The app is optional but recommended. It works offline, holds your saved sessions, and records QR stamps for attendance. You can install it from the Event app page.

When are the pre-conference workshops?

The pre-conference workshops take place on {{venue.preConference.label}}, the day before the main meeting, in the Salon, 20/F, Crystal Court.

Will sessions be recorded?

Selected scientific sessions are planned for OnDemand after the meeting. There is no live stream, and workshops, social functions and ceremonies are not recorded.

How does Stamp Collection work?

Each eligible session has a QR code in the room. Open the event app, choose Stamp Collection, and scan the code. Collect at least 7 of the 8 slot stamps to claim a souvenir. If you prefer not to use the app, a paper stamp card is available on request at the registration desk, 19/F; electronic and paper stamps both count. See the About the ASM page for details.

CME/CPD and certificates

Will CPD points be available?

Accreditation is being arranged with the relevant colleges and professional bodies. Only confirmed points are published on the CPD page.

How do I get a certificate of attendance?

An evaluation survey is sent after the meeting. Complete it to receive your e-Certificate of Attendance by email.

How do I claim CME/CPD points?

Follow the claiming process set by your college or employer. Your certificate of attendance and programme record support the claim. Confirmed accreditation is listed on the CME/CPD page.

Accessibility and family

Is the venue accessible?

The venue has lifts and step-free access to the meeting floors. See the Accessibility page for step-free access, seating and assistance.

Is there a parents' or carers' room?

A dedicated parents' and carers' room within the ASM meeting space is not confirmed. See the Accessibility page for the facilities available and ask the registration desk for advice.

Are dietary requirements catered for?

Note dietary requirements during registration. The venue labels food where possible and provides alternatives for common requirements. For severe allergies, tell the registration team before the meeting so the catering team can be briefed. See the Accessibility page.

Sponsors

How do I exhibit or sponsor?

Download the 2027 sponsorship package PDF from the Sponsorship page, then use the Contact page to apply. We will confirm availability and send the contract.

What sponsorship packages are available?

The full package list, inclusions and investment levels are in the 2027 sponsorship package PDF on the Sponsorship page. Availability is confirmed on receipt of the signed contract.

What is included in an exhibition booth?

Exhibition booths include a table, two chairs and an exhibitor listing. Final specifications are confirmed with the organiser.

Can we choose our booth position?

Booth allocation is confirmed on receipt of the signed contract.

Is there an exhibitor manual?

There is no separate exhibitor manual. Build-up, dismantling and booth logistics are confirmed directly with the organiser.

Can we run a lecture or workshop?

Named lectures and workshops are available as sponsorship packages, subject to scientific committee approval and programme space.

How do we submit artwork?

Artwork requirements and deadlines are confirmed by email after your application is accepted.

Who do we contact about sponsorship?

Use the Contact page with the package you are interested in.

Can all delegates visit the exhibition?

Yes. Registered delegates can visit the healthcare industry exhibition during the meeting.
