Terms and conditions

Registration

Each participant must complete their own registration form. Registration is confirmed only when full payment is received and a confirmation email is issued. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide.

Fees and payment

Fees are in Hong Kong dollars. Payment methods will be listed on the registration page. A registration without payment will not be processed. Please do not send cash.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations must be made in writing to the meeting secretariat. The refund scale is:

Date of cancellationRefund
On or before {{ePosterFreePaper.presentingDeadline}}50% of the registration fee
After {{ePosterFreePaper.presentingDeadline}}No refund

Refunds are processed after the meeting. Workshop, social and optional event tickets may have separate terms. Refunds are not provided for unsuccessful visa applications.

Substitutions

Substitutions are considered on written request before the meeting. The substitute must meet the eligibility requirements for the registration category.

Changes to the programme

The programme, speakers, rooms and social functions may change. The organisers will make reasonable efforts to notify registered delegates of material changes but do not guarantee a particular speaker or session.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, the organisers are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, travel or accommodation costs, or loss arising from events outside their reasonable control. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

Force majeure

If the meeting cannot be held as planned because of circumstances outside the organisers' control, the organisers may reschedule, change the format or provide alternative access. Registration fees will be handled in a fair and transparent way, as announced at the time.

Conduct and safety

All participants must follow the venue's safety rules. The organisers may refuse entry or remove a participant who breaches these terms or poses a risk to others.

Intellectual property

Presenters retain responsibility for their content and must have permission to use any third-party material. By presenting, you grant the organisers a non-exclusive licence to record, publish and archive your session content for meeting purposes, subject to any separate agreement.
