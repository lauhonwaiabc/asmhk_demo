HKCA Congregation Ceremony

About the ceremony

The HKCA Congregation is the College's annual ceremony for new Fellows in Anaesthesiology and Intensive Care, Fellows in Pain Medicine, and award recipients. It is held alongside {{event.shortName}} and is followed by the Conference / Congregation Dinner.

Schedule

Before the ceremony

Gowns. Collect your gown from {{ceremony.gownCollection}}, before the rehearsal; gowns are issued on a first-come-first-served basis. Wear the gown for the rehearsal. If you keep the gown, have it dry cleaned and return it to the College by the date advised.

Rehearsal. The rehearsal takes about 15 minutes and explains the presentation of fellowships. Attendance is strongly encouraged.

Photographs. A photo backdrop is available in the {{ceremony.details.photoBoothArea}} on {{ceremony.details.photoBoothFloor}}, {{venue.name}}. Allow time before or after the ceremony.

Guests and families

Guest seating is on a first-come-first-served basis. Please ask guests to be seated by {{ceremony.guestsSeatedBy}}. Families are welcome; parents are asked to help children observe ceremony etiquette. College staff can assist anyone who needs to step out briefly.

Certificates and dinner

Certificate folders are distributed after the ceremony; collect yours before joining the reception and dinner. The Conference / Congregation Dinner follows at {{ceremony.dinner.time}} in {{ceremony.dinner.venue}}.

Getting there and parking

The ceremony is at {{venue.name}}, {{venue.street}}. Wan Chai MTR Station (Exit D) is about a 3–5 minute walk via Hopewell Mall; taxis and cars arrive at Level 18. The public car park is on 14/F–16/F of Hopewell Mall. See Venue and Travel & visa.

Contact

For Congregation arrangements, contact the College office at (852) 2871 8768 or see hkca.edu.hk. The College will circulate an on-the-day contact number to graduands.
