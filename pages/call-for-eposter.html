Call for Free Paper and e-Poster

Free Paper and e-Poster

One abstract. Two presentation options.

Judges review all accepted e-Posters at the Day 1 e-Poster Evaluation Session on {{event.day1Long}}, 14:00–15:30, Pre-function area. Only the best e-Posters whose authors opted for Free Paper consideration at submission proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation on {{event.day2Long}}, 11:00–12:30, Salon 1–2. The outcome is announced on site after the evaluation. High-quality e-Posters that did not opt for Free Paper may still be invited to present in the Day 1 evaluation session.

Two options at submission

01

e-Poster only

Your abstract is accepted and displayed as an e-Poster in the website gallery, app and on-site e-Poster area. It is not eligible for the Free Paper Presentation. A high-quality e-Poster may still be invited to present in the Day 1 e-Poster Evaluation Session.

02

e-Poster + Free Paper consideration

Your accepted e-Poster is displayed in the gallery and reviewed by judges on Day 1. If it is one of the best e-Posters in the evaluation, you proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation.

Declarations

  • Ethics approval and patient consent must be stated where applicable. Abstracts without appropriate approvals will not be considered.
  • Declare any conflict of interest, funding or prior presentation at submission.
  • Remove patient-identifiable information and obtain consent for clinical images.
  • Work must be original or a substantive service evaluation. Case reports may be considered at the committee's discretion.

Before you submit

  • Submit in English through the online portal. The abstract limit is 300 words, with up to one table or figure.
  • Each presenting author may submit up to two abstracts.
  • Every abstract that meets the guidelines is accepted as an e-Poster.
  • At submission, choose either e-Poster only or e-Poster plus consideration for the Free Paper Presentation.
  • All accepted e-Posters are reviewed by judges on Day 1. Only the best e-Posters whose authors chose Free Paper consideration can proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation. High-quality e-Posters without Free Paper consideration may be invited to present in the Day 1 evaluation session.
  • The presenting author must register as a paid delegate by the stated deadline for the abstract to remain in the programme.
  • Notification and format allocation are sent by email to the presenting author.
  • All accepted abstracts are published in the e-programme or abstract book, subject to author consent.
  • Case reports may be considered at the Committee's discretion. Patient-identifiable information must be removed.
  • Work must be original or a substantive service evaluation. State any conflict of interest and prior presentation.

How it works

  1. Submit one abstract and choose your optionUse the online abstract form. Choose e-Poster only, or e-Poster plus consideration for the Free Paper Presentation.
  2. Compliance checkEvery abstract that meets the guidelines is accepted as an e-Poster. The check covers structure, consent, disclosure and patient confidentiality.
  3. Prepare the e-PosterAfter acceptance, upload a single-page PDF in the supplied template by the stated deadline.
  4. Publish and displaye-Posters appear in the website gallery, the app and the on-site e-Poster area with a searchable title, authors, affiliation and category.
  5. Day 1: e-Poster Evaluation SessionOn {{event.day1Long}}, 14:00–15:30 in the Pre-function area, judges review all accepted e-Posters. High-quality e-Posters without Free Paper consideration may be invited to present during this session.
  6. Selection for Day 2From the e-Posters whose authors chose Free Paper consideration, judges select the best to proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation. The outcome is announced on site after the Day 1 evaluation.
  7. Day 2: Free Paper PresentationSelected presenters present orally on {{event.day2Long}}, 11:00–12:30 in Salon 1–2. Prizes are announced at the Closing Ceremony.

Free Paper Presentation

The Free Paper Presentation is the oral stage for the best e-Posters from the Day 1 evaluation whose authors opted for Free Paper consideration at submission. All accepted e-Posters are reviewed by judges on Day 1; only the best eligible e-Posters proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation. There is no separate abstract submission for the Free Paper session.

Oral format

  • Oral presentation in Salon 1–2 on {{event.day2Long}}, 11:00–12:30.
  • Ten minutes for the presentation and five minutes for questions.
  • Standard projection is 16:9. Upload your slides at the speaker preview desk before the session.
  • Presentations should follow the e-Poster abstract: question, methods, results and conclusion.
  • A chairperson and timekeeper will keep the session to schedule.
  • Prizes are announced at the Closing Ceremony on Day 2.

Evaluation and selection

  • Judges review all accepted e-Posters at the Day 1 e-Poster Evaluation Session, {{event.day1Long}}, 14:00–15:30, Pre-function area.
  • The panel scores e-Posters on quality, relevance, clarity and originality.
  • Only the best e-Posters whose authors chose Free Paper consideration at submission proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation.
  • High-quality e-Posters that did not opt for Free Paper may be invited to present in the Day 1 evaluation session.
  • The outcome is announced on site after the Day 1 evaluation; it is not published in the static app.
  • Authors who chose e-Poster only remain in the e-Poster gallery if they are not invited to present during the evaluation session.
  • Final instructions are sent by email to selected presenters.

What the panel looks for

  • Scientific quality and originality
  • Relevance to anaesthesia, intensive care, pain or perioperative medicine
  • Clarity of the question, methods and conclusions
  • Likely value to the ASM audience
  • All accepted e-Posters are displayed. Only the best e-Posters from authors who chose Free Paper consideration can proceed to the Day 2 Free Paper Presentation; high-quality e-Posters without Free Paper consideration may be invited to present in the evaluation session.

Prizes

  • Best Free Paper Presentation, selected from the Day 2 Free Paper presenters.
  • Certificates for all presenters.

e-Poster guidelines

  • One page, A0 portrait proportions (841 – 1189 mm), exported as PDF at 150 dpi or higher.
  • Include title, authors, affiliations, introduction, methods, results, conclusion and disclosure.
  • Use the {{event.shortName}} e-Poster template. Keep body text at least 24 pt and headings at least 36 pt.
  • Remove patient-identifiable information and obtain consent for any clinical image.
  • Maximum file size 20 MB. Name the file with your abstract number, for example ASM27-0123.pdf.
  • Audio is not accepted. Submit a static PDF e-Poster only.

Awards

  • Best Free Paper Presentation, selected from the Day 2 Free Paper presenters
