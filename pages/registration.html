Registration

Registrants are advised to read the registration information carefully before submitting.

Registration categories

Registration categories and rates for the scientific symposia. All amounts are in Hong Kong Dollars.

A verification letter from the Department of Anaesthesiology in your hospital is required to qualify for the trainee fee.
Hong Kong Medical Student / Intern registrants are entitled to the Scientific Symposia and Healthcare Industry Exhibition. The Nursing Symposium is not included. Please submit a copy of a valid Student ID / Intern ID with registration.
Registrants are entitled to the Scientific Symposia, Healthcare Industry Exhibition, tea breaks and lunches. Limited lunch symposium seats are available on a first-come-first-served basis. The Nursing Symposium is not included.

What registration includes

  • Access to scientific sessions on the days registered
  • Healthcare industry exhibition
  • Tea breaks and lunches
  • E-Programme and abstract book
  • Certificate of attendance, issued with attendance
  • Temporary luggage storage at the registration desk, 19/F

Group registration

Hospital departments and institutions can register a group. Send the number of delegates, categories and any workshop choices to the Meeting Secretariat, and we will send a group form and invoice.

Nursing Symposium

The Nursing Symposium will be held on {{event.day1}}.

Optional workshops

Workshops are allocated on a first-come-first-served basis; PBL Workshops 1 and 2 are separate bookings.

Conference & Congregation Dinner

One complimentary ticket to each {{event.shortName}} registrant except Students, Interns, Day registrants and Nursing Symposium registrants is planned. As seats are limited, tickets are allocated on a first-come-first-served basis.

Payment

Payment must be made in Hong Kong Dollars (HK$) by the following methods:

  1. Cheque: payable to “The Hong Kong College of Anaesthesiologists”.
  2. Credit card: Visa / Mastercard / Alipay.

Registration confirmation will be emailed to the registered delegate after receipt of full payment.

Rules and regulations

  1. Each registrant should complete a separate online registration form.
  2. A registration form without payment will not be processed. Please do not send cash.
  3. Any changes must be made in writing.
  4. The programme is subject to change without prior notice. In the event of cancellation of the meeting, the Organisers’ liability is limited to refunding all fees paid.
  5. Workshops may be cancelled due to insufficient enrolment.
  6. Personal data supplied for registration will be used only by the Organisers and related working parties, subject to applicable data protection law.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations must be made in writing to the Meeting Secretariat. The refund scale is:

Date of cancellationRefund
On or before {{ePosterFreePaper.presentingDeadline}}50% of the registration fee
After {{ePosterFreePaper.presentingDeadline}}No refund

Refunds are processed after the meeting.
