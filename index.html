Welcome Message

Dear Colleagues and Friends,

Welcome to the {{event.name}} — an international congress on {{event.theme}}.

Intelligence in anaesthesia has never been only artificial. It is the judgement built from training and experience, the situational awareness of a team, and the data that now surrounds every decision. {{event.shortName}} explores where these intelligences meet: what the evidence supports, what changes practice, and what remains a matter of human judgement.

Across two days we combine plenary sessions with focused symposia, hands-on workshops and the Nursing Symposium, alongside the HKCA Congregation and the Conference / Congregation Dinner. The programme is designed for anaesthesiologists, trainees, nurses and allied health professionals from Hong Kong, the mainland, Asia-Pacific and beyond, and the international programme brings perspectives from across the specialty together in one place.

We look forward to welcoming you to Hong Kong.

Dr. CHEE Yee EotPresident
The Hong Kong College of Anaesthesiologists

Why attend

Evidence you can use

Plenary lectures and focused symposia separate what is ready for practice from what still needs evidence, with time to test the arguments against your own service.

Practical workshops

Small-group, hands-on sessions on airway, regional anaesthesia, POCUS, echocardiography, AI and communication — designed to change what you do on the next list.

An international community

Meet anaesthesiologists, trainees, nurses and allied health professionals from Hong Kong, the mainland, Asia-Pacific and beyond, alongside the HKCA Congregation and social programme.
